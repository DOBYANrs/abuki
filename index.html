Delivery and payment

Simple terms, written down before you pay.

Payment

We accept Telebirr and CBE Birr. All prices are in ETB. You pay 50% after accepting a quotation and 50% when your order arrives in Addis Ababa, before delivery.

Delivery

Sea shipping from China takes about 2–3 months. Delivery is free in Addis Ababa. Other Ethiopian towns are quoted by location.

Refunds

The 50% deposit is refundable if you cancel before we order from the supplier. After we order, it is non-refundable.
