How it works

Every order has its own Order ID and tracking page.

Product in the shop

  1. Choose the product and order it.
  2. Pay the full price by Telebirr or CBE.
  3. Send us a photo of your receipt.
  4. We confirm, order from China and ship.
  5. We deliver to you.

Product not in the shop

  1. Send us a photo of the product.
  2. We check it and send you a price.
  3. Accept the price and pay 50%.
  4. We order and ship it.
  5. Pay the other 50% when it arrives. We deliver.
