Delivery and payment

Simple terms, written down before you pay.

Payment

We accept Telebirr and CBE. All prices are in ETB. You pay 50% after accepting a quotation and 50% when your order arrives in Addis Ababa, before delivery.

Delivery

By air: about 7–14 days. By sea: about 2–3 months, at a lower cost. Delivery is free in Addis Ababa. Other Ethiopian towns are quoted by location.

Refunds

The 50% deposit is refundable if you cancel before we order from the supplier. After we order, it is non-refundable.
